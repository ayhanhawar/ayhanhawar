01 — About

Two disciplines. One habit: understand it, then explain it.

I'm from Soran, and I'm now in stage 2 of dentistry at Knowledge University in Erbil. Dentistry rewards patience and millimetre accuracy — the same thing I like about building AR filters and cutting video.

Through 2025 I ran a high-volume printing and copying business at Soran University, handling thousands of full-colour pages a day. I've since closed that chapter to focus on my degree — but deadlines, margins, and fixing things under pressure came with me.

Alongside my studies I still create — tech tutorials, gadget reviews, and Snapchat Lenses — sharing what I learn from Kurdistan with anyone who'll watch. The plan is to keep both tracks running: clinical training on one side, building and teaching on the other.

07 — FAQ

Questions I get asked

How did you get started with Snapchat Lens creation?

It started as a hobby in my free time. The response to my first few lenses was better than I expected, so I went deeper into AR development — and I've been building more complex, more interactive lenses ever since.

What tools do you use for your content?

Adobe Premiere Pro and Final Cut Pro for video. Snapchat Lens Studio for AR, with Spark AR now and then. VS Code for anything involving code.

How do you balance dentistry with content creation?

Dentistry is the priority now that I'm in stage 2, so creating happens in fixed blocks around lectures and labs. I also try to make the two feed each other, turning what I'm studying into material worth explaining.

Do you take freelance work?

Yes — graphic design and video editing, taken on selectively around my study schedule. I no longer run the print shop. Send me a message below with what you need and a deadline.
